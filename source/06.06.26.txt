

A wise man once told me to never look Eagle’s in the eye. Fly high over the mountain tops amongst the clouds. Soft silk in the cottage on the river ferry. The ripples on the water shines, and the light dances on the top. Crops, trimmed and ready. Diamond tipped pencil on some lined paper. 

I see her in my dreams sometimes, I met her at the castle on the hill. We spoke about all things good and evil. Her smile reminded me of the time before the sun rises. The light was soft when the wind blew her away- I could barely see. The moss on the stones to my right whisper of the silent night. Silent nights, a silent knight, checkmate. Tip toe-ing to see the other side, the grass is always greener. I stood back and observed from afar. I remember that’s what I called Ismail’s sister from school. The cliff drops of quickly. I close my eyes as my feet dangle and find myself on the back of a dragon in the sky. Once they’re back open, I see her in the distance - dancing in the distance on a whiles back in the waves. She glows white in the moonlight. She glows white like moonlight. I wander why,. I wander who sent you to find me and what I’m supposed to see. Am I supposed to see? I sometimes feel like a blind man with no blindfold in the late night. I say hi to the ghosts as they cross the bridge. Some friendly spirits and some grumpy ones. They would pray for my downfall but they don’t pray at all. I wish we all prayed a little more.

A mouse scurries across the green grass. I heard it’s soft footsteps and short-breaths in the brush as I walked by. He has his midnight meeting on the 12th day of the cycle at the old Oak tree, so long my friend. He was wearing his finest little Tuxedo. The sleeves ran too long and the bow-tie was crooked. He was in a rush and his pocket-watch was late. 

Fate greets me at the gate. It’s been some time since we last met - she’s growing old. 
Her cold glare doesn’t cut me anymore.

“Why was I here again?”
“We’re all here and gone and gone again.
What’s the date my child? Matter of fact, have you got a pen?”

I pass her the tip of my tongue to write my cards.
‘jack of all trades, with an Ace up your sleeve’

“I don’t want those ones anymore”
I couldn’t actually say it without my tongue, but my eyes told it anyways.

Her’s scolded me in response.



‘1 visitors pass to Tomorrow’
‘- No talking of Yesterday’s promises
- No physical torches
- all bags must be stored under your eyes
- No small children
- All black cats must be registered with the Mage under the Willow’

‘ Follow the glowing mushrooms to the cloakroom where you can store your memories for the journey.

 A reminder that all dreams are FINAL and cannot be returned after issue.’



I waited some time at the street-lamp in the strawberry field. The lamp stood still for most of my wait. I could sense him getting tired when he would start to blink slowly and lean to the side slightly. His warm orange glow would fade to a softer hue before he stands up straight again.


My ride arrives at 00:68. Only a couple minutes to late, I should get going.

I’m in a hurry now because I don’t want to miss the trailers. People always call them boring or think that they’re skippable, but they get me excited - I know it’s not long before the show begins. I never found out why they’re called trailer but always precede the dream. Perhaps it’s me who’s trailing?


The curtains draw. This time it’s three little silver-suit wearing Otter’s doing the introduction. 

The left-most one stares into the distance the whole time and doesn’t speak. He’s about 15 centimeters too far away from the central Otter, and he faces outwards slightly too much, as if he’s searching for someone in the crowd. It can’t be someone in the crowd he’s looking for though; I study the glint in the glare on his beady eyes and watch the memories with him. 

He misses her much. He’s remembering the night she met him under the apple tree two streams away from his favourite fishing spot, they always met there on nights like this. He’s not really a big reader, but when he sees her - he feels inspired to start after watching her read the stars for some time.


“ … and so it is with great pleasure that we introduce the cast of tonight’s dream.”
	- the middle Otter with a red hanker-chief folded into his breast pocket

 * applause ensues *

Anyways, I take a sip of my reminisces in a Martini glass. They leave the pits in the olives here, where should I put it?





The flamingos are coming onto the stage now. Their canvas is layered.

They’ve cut three of four sheets of card in different colours and have them situated at different heights. I can’t tell if the turquise one is moving or not - it seems to be swaying a little bit, maybe that’s part of it. 

This drink is strong.

The flamingo’s dance for a bit. They turn and prance about but never face the audience. The metaphorical sun drifts accross the sky and the moon follows him. 

She looks slightly sad today. Someone told me the Wolf’s don’t cry to her anymore, she must be lonely these days. She can’t even talk to the stars anymore, they’ve all moved out now.

I can feel myself drifting off now.

I’ll pick up my jacket, fold it onto my left arm and get up and walk.

Yes, that’s what I’ll do.

They’ll understand, surely they won’t be offended.



The show Was great, besides I’ve seen it before.



I bump into a gecko with a top-hat on my way out of my row.

I know I’m safe and I drift off once again.
