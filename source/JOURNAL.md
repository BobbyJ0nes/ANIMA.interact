# the journal, where it touches this night

the piece is built from the author's journal. the spine — the dream-entry
06.06.26 — ships whole in this folder. the entries below are the others
the night draws on, excerpted verbatim, typos kept. the elisions `[…]`
are deliberate: only the passages related to this work are included; the
journal beyond them stays private. the small arrows are the only editorial
voice — they say where each passage lives in the piece.

---

## solipsist.txt (undated, spring 26)

> One thing that I can be certain of is overlap between cultures and
> concepts which elude to this premise of what Jung calls the collective
> subconscious, what religion calls Destiny and what other
> interpretations denote in other ways such as energy, time phases and
> astrological significance.

→ the force Fate personifies — why she keeps the gate.

> […] One thing I want to avoid with this project is to force the
> subconscious data into a specific shape, doing so would impart the
> conscious into the work.

→ the method's central refusal: the subconscious as weather, never
content; the unshaped ground; honesty labels on every signal.
