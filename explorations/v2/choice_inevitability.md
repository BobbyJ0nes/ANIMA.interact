## 0 · the principle

Choices in this night are never branches. They are **who you were while
the same thing happened**. Every stage still ends where night.js says it
ends; what a choice changes is *company, flavor, and what the next night
remembers*. Three species only:

- **false forks** — two paths, one destination (spatial: which way west)
- **refusable offers** — you can say no; the night absorbs the refusal
  and advances anyway; the refusal is kept
- **timed inevitabilities** — things that happen regardless, visibly
  stamped (00:68 arrives whether or not you waited well)

No new input surfaces except one cursor (the menu — see stage 4). No
prompts, no "press X". The margin's canon lines stay the night's only
instructions. Choices are made with the body: where you stand, how long,
whether you move.

## 1 · state — `dream.chosen` / `dream.echo`

```js
// engine.js dream object, persisted with the rest
dream.chosen = {         // this night, written as it happens
  tongue: 'gave'|'withheld',
  trail:  'mushrooms'|'water'|'dark',
  coat:   'tried'|'passed',
  cats:   'named'|'unregistered',
  lamp:   'kept'|'left',
  order:  'entree'|'main'|'desert'|'catch'|'none',
  show:   'left-early'|'sat-long',
  ledge:  'looked'|'leapt-blind',
  sea:    'still'|'rowed',
}
dream.echo = {}          // LAST night's chosen — the only thing echoes read
```

At the fold (night.js): `markDream('echo', {...dream.chosen});
markDream('chosen', {});` — obligations reset (pass, jacket), conduct is
remembered. **Echoes are therefore silent on the first night and only
become audible on the second** — recurrence made mechanical. First-time
walkers get a clean canon night; the returning dreamer is greeted by how
they behaved.

## 2 · the choice map, stage by stage

### stage 0 — the gate
1. **the tongue** *(refusable offer)*. Fate asks for a pen. Approach the
   grip → the arc plays as built → `tongue:'gave'`. Withhold (stand off
   beyond ~20s with the fate column cued) → the pass condenses **anyway**;
   the margin types *"The Roulette table wobbles, Someone paid off
   Destiny -"* (20.07.26.txt:15 — cross-entry, see §5) →
   `tongue:'withheld'`. Either way `dream.pass = true`. The choice was
   only ever *who paid*. Echo: a withheld night makes next night's
   greeting scold-first ("Her's scolded me in response" arrives before
   the greeting lines).
2. **the way west** *(false fork)*. Mushrooms (canon) · the south water
   (the wake's rings also run west) · the dark north meadow. All satisfy
   the stage: two extra trailEnd triggers (west edge reached by water or
   by dark). Going dark, the mushrooms **relocate into your path** — new
   pulse points seed on your actual line, and the margin types the canon
   instruction ("Follow the glowing mushrooms to the cloakroom…") — the
   night insisting on its own text. `trail` recorded; next night the
   willow cats approach from your trail's side.

### stage 1 — the cloakroom
1. **try a coat / pass** *(refusable offer)*. Dwell beside one coat > 3s
   → it's too large — margin types *"Each jacket slightly larger than the
   next for when I inevitably out grow it."* (17.08.26.txt:24 — the
   cloakroom IS his closet of constructed identities, in his own words) →
   `coat:'tried'`. Pass straight through → nothing, now. Echo: next
   night, the coat you wore **pauses its sway** as you pass — it
   remembers being worn. The aisle still has one far mouth; both sides
   converge; z<-42 regardless.

### stage 2 — the willow
1. **register the black cats or don't** *(both rewarded — the purest
   illusion)*. Stand at the mage's book with cats present → `'named'` →
   the cats walk you to the road's west edge. Ignore it → `'unregistered'`
   → a black cat crosses your path at the streetlamp and sits in the sag
   light with you. Company changes; the road doesn't. (The pass rules
   are the dream's own design doc; this is rule 5 enacted.)

### stage 3 — the streetlamp
1. **keep him company / pace away** *(timed inevitability)*. Kept (sag
   witnessed, current mechanic) → the ride stops at the lamp, door held.
   Left (beyond radius > 8s during the wait) → **the ride still arrives
   at 00:68** — it stops farther down the road, holds the door shorter;
   you run for it. Same margin line either way: "Only a couple minutes
   to late, I should get going." 00:68 is the schedule of inevitability
   — a time that doesn't exist, kept perfectly, every night, printed
   regardless of conduct.

### stage 4 — the theatre
1. **the order** *(the emblem-choice)*. While the menu is down: A/D
   moves a '·' cursor among Entree / Main / Desert / Catch; Space
   orders; 16s of silence orders nothing (`'none'`, they decide). What
   arrives is **always the same**: "I take a sip of my reminisces in a
   Martini glass." A menu — the civilization-symbol of choice — that
   serves the one drink. The order echoes at the boat only: `'catch'` →
   the rod from the emergency pack glints brighter; `'desert'` → the sea
   letters near the boat dry toward ramp-dots; others → a margin nod
   next fold. The cursor is the night's ONLY new input surface, and it
   exists in order not to matter.
2. **sit the whole thing out** *(inevitability by boredom)*. Don't stand
   at the drift cue → the show **reruns** — flamingos again, and the
   margin types the canon "The show Was great, besides I've seen it
   before." — then re-arms the drift cue. Sit forever; the night waits,
   playing reruns. Leaving early is never forced; it becomes the
   interactor's own restlessness — they enact the writing themselves.
   `show:'sat-long'` if ≥1 rerun witnessed.

### stage 5 — ledge & boat
1. **look down first** *(canon beat made optional)*. Pitch down at the
   ledge > 3s → the rock-toss plays ("I toss a rock below / There's
   never a splash") → `ledge:'looked'`. Leap without looking →
   `'leapt-blind'` — the splash-refusal is met in the sea itself.
2. **row / be still** *(the last refusal)*. WASD near the boat = rowing;
   **the boat does not move** — this sea writes no wakes for anyone.
   `sea:'rowed'`; the fold still only comes through stillness (canon).
   Echo: a rowed night earns, at the next gate, the margin line *"Let it
   be what it always was."* (20.07.26.txt:18) — resignation you paid for.

## 3 · inevitability made visible — six showings of the hand

1. **00:68** — the impossible time, kept perfectly, both conducts.
2. **the menu that serves reminisces** whatever you order.
3. **mushrooms that relocate** into a disobedient path, then repeat
   their instruction verbatim.
4. **"Someone paid off Destiny"** — the pass arriving unpaid.
5. **the reruns** — sitting forever earns only "I've seen it before."
6. **the card exchange replays a refusal that already happened** — the
   canon already contains "I don't want those ones anymore" and the
   scold; whatever the interactor does at the grip, they are replaying
   an argument the writing lost before they arrived. (Stage the three
   condensed lines in strict order; no interactivity — that's the point.)

## 4 · the liminal dial

One scalar, engine-owned, authored:

```js
// L: 0 at the first gate, ~0.7 at the first boat, floor rises per fold
export function liminal() {
  const stage = director ? director.stageIdx() : 0;
  const L = (stage / 5) * 0.7 + Math.min(dream.folds || 0, 3) * 0.1;
  return labMode ? 0 : Math.min(1, L);
}
```

Couplings — all existing dials, gains only, no new systems:

| dial | coupling | cap |
|---|---|---|
| fog | fogK × (1 + 0.35·L) | ×1.35 |
| letterland | LL + 0.25·L·(1−LL) — land dissolves toward text | +0.25 |
| stars | depl = max(0.08, 1 − 0.75·L − 0.09·extraVisits) — the depletion arc becomes the dial's arc; the boat's emptied sky is where L was always going | floor 0.08 |
| wind | gust amp × (1 + 0.4·L·tension) | ×1.4 |
| ramp | at L>0.6, rampCeil −1 — '█▓' withdraw, the world de-solidifies | −1 |
| margin | cps 26 → 18 as L rises; letters occasionally settle one cell adrift then correct (h2i jitter < 0.15·L) — the voice gets sleepy, **the words stay verbatim** | cps ≥ 18 |
| transitions | dur × (1 + 0.5·L) — dissolutions linger | ×1.5 |

**Considered and rejected: register drift toward veil.** The six-register
identity is the piece's visual argument; liminality lives in the dials,
never the palette. Also rejected: person-shift i→you in the margin — it
rewrites his sentences, and verbatim is locked. The falter does the same
work without touching a word.

Lab mode: L = 0 always (process view stays clinical); `?liminal=0.8`
override for testing only.

## 6 · eeg honesty — authored arc, bodily weather

The liminal arc is **narrative time** (stage-indexed, authored). The env
channels are **body time** (MODEL oscillators or the RECORDED session).
They multiply and never impersonate: L scales gains on env couplings;
env never moves L. Labeling: the HUD marker gains `· arc` in dream mode
(or PROCESS.md alone documents it — bobby's call which). LIVE (the
unopened door), if ever opened: a live session may lend at most ±0.1 to
L (long-average of depth), disclosed on screen — the arc stays authored;
the body borrows the margin. Contract: `L_eff = clamp01(L_authored +
0.1·(depth_60s − 0.5)·liveEnabled)`, label `LIVE lends to the arc`.

## 8 · risks

1. **choice-UI creep** — guard: one cursor, zero prompts; every other
   choice is positional/temporal.
2. **cross-entry provenance** — 3 lines max, each cited, each awaiting
   his yes; silent fallback ready.
3. **echo inaudibility** — echoes are whisper-quiet by design; they are
   for the second night and the report. Accepted.
4. **liminality vs readability** — caps above are hard; the playthrough
   must pass at folds=3 with L at ceiling.
5. **the rerun loop** — must never soft-lock: standing is always
   allowed; `until` stays `signals.leapt`.
6. **first-night purity** — echoes read `dream.echo` only, so a fresh
   walk (?fresh) is exactly the canon night. Guard this in the
   playthrough (assert no echo lines on night one).
