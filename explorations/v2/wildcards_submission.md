## 1 · wildcard deck — ten ideas, ten layers, ranked

each: the idea, its root, cost (S/M/L), the risk.

### w1 · the reader — the faded hand (choice grammar)  [cost M]

the night's choice mechanic, transcribed from 17.08.26: at each
decision point a **faded hand** condenses — dim, half-there, offering
(the card at the gate · the aisle at the cloakroom · the door of the
ride · the menu · the stairs). walk into it = take it. walk away or
wait = decline. **declining never blocks the night** — the formed hand
comes eventually and pulls (the ride stops again at the next 00:68;
fate's grip waits; the stairs float regardless) — but the world
remembers every declined hand: tension rises, the margin's tone at the
next threshold shifts, the fold marker keeps its own count. illusion
of choice, inevitability of encounter — not as game-design pattern but
as testimony: this is how the author describes his own waking
mechanism. *root:* 17.08.26 verbatim. *risk:* legibility of a hand in
character-forms — needs one clear condensed shape ( `(` `/` `)` fingers )
taught once at the gate where the entry already has hands (the pen-hand,
the grip).

### w2 · structure — the six paragraphs (chapter folios)  [cost M]

the director's six stages ARE the paragraphs of 06.06.26. open each
stage with its verbatim paragraph as a **folio chapter page**: the
world dissolves (transition already ends in letters), the letters
settle into the paragraph typed in his hand's order, holds until read
or walked (walking tears the page — the world condenses out of the
paragraph's own letters). provenance becomes the story's own
structure: you always know where you are and why, because the page
told you — then the place proves it. *root:* the entry's own paragraph
breaks; PROCESS.md's "the third dimension is an act of typesetting."
*risk:* pacing — reading gates walking. guard: pages are interruptible
everywhere; the full text also lives on as the ground-narrator.

### w3 · medium — the letter (tufayl's frame)  [cost S]

the piece admits what it is: **a letter sent to you.** before the
gate, one folio page — the entry's opening line typing as if being
written now, addressed outward ("A wise man once told me…" · a
diamond-tipped pencil on some lined paper — the entry's own first
paragraph contains its writing instrument). after the fold, the page
refolds — the dream "folds" the way a letter folds. the night's
question "why am I here" gets its sharpest form: *you were sent this.*
"I wander who sent you to find me and what I'm supposed to see" — the
entry already wonders who sent whom. *root:* solipsist.txt's Hayy Ibn
Yaqzaan passage (a story written as a letter to a friend, to carry
what can't be said directly); 06.06.26 ¶1. *risk:* literary-clever if
it spreads; keep it to the two bookends only.

### w4 · the machine — the second dreamer breathes  [cost S/M]

the EEG stops being weather-only and becomes a **presence**: in
RECORDED mode the whole frame carries a slow luminance swell at the
recorded session's own slow rhythm — a body breathing under the
world — and once per night the margin states the honesty label:
`recorded · 06.06.26 · 74 minutes`. you are walking a written night
inside a recorded one; two dreamers, one room. at the crossing, add
the third witness: **your own clock** — "you crossed at 00:xx" stamped
beside the poster's ~28 and the pipeline's ~52. three clocks, none
agreeing — the piece's whole epistemology in one place. *root:*
session01.json; the honesty taxonomy; the crossing verdict ("the
disagreement is the honesty"). *risk:* too subtle to read as intent —
the one label is what teaches it; keep the label.

### w5 · identity — the misfit i  [cost S]

the margin says "i" while you act. two or three times, late in the
night, let the "i" act **without you**: the margin types "i toss a
rock below" — and a rock falls whether or not you threw one. you
catch the world performing its text. am i doing the dream or is the
dream doing me — the exact unsure the brief asks for ("or at least get
you questioning that"). *root:* 31.07.26 — "all of my memories seem to
have formed separately. As if I had been alone - in company";
visitation-with-misrecognition (locked). *risk:* overuse reads as
broken agency. budget: three moments, all past the theatre
(the liminal gradient earns them).

### w6 · time — the dream-clock runs to 00:68  [cost S/M]

the night has its own clock, and it is wrong the way dreams are
wrong: numerals in the writing's alphabet, surfacing **only at
thresholds** (never persistent): `the gate · 00:07`. it ticks
non-uniformly — env-coupled (tension quickens it, depth slows it) —
and the only time it ever agrees with anything is the ride's arrival:
00:68. "Only a couple minutes to late." the mouse's pocket-watch is
late; the lamp's cycle is the only honest metronome. *root:* the
entry's 00:68 + the mouse's watch. *risk:* HUD-creep — guard: numerals
appear at stage arrivals and the boarding only, then scatter.

### w7 · the fold — marginalia accrete  [cost M]

fold 2+ : the night remembers being read. places you lingered last
pass carry faint reader's marks — an underline beneath the ground-
paragraph where you stood longest, a dog-ear fold in the corner of a
chapter folio, the trail more worn (worn trails already exist). never
words — marks only. a book read twice; your previous reading is the
ghost in it. *root:* "We're all here and gone and gone again"; 31.07's
sequential-night dreams blending; the depletion arc already drains
stars per visit. *risk:* invisible to one-pass markers — acceptable;
it's for the author and for returners (and it documents itself in the
process record).

### w8 · the room — the trailers are the boot-log  [cost S]

the theatre's trailers (the entry: "I never found out why they're
called trailer but always precede the dream. Perhaps it's me who's
trailing?") are the piece's own machinery, shown once, diegetically:
before the otters, the screen runs **previews of the world's own
making** — `the strawberry field · 28,000 glyphs` · `the moon,
typeset` · `tonight's sky: recorded` — the renderer introducing
itself as coming attractions. the dream knows it's a show ("The show
Was great, besides I've seen it before"). *root:* the entry's trailer
paragraph; PROCESS.md's renderer description. *risk:* fourth-wall
puncture — but the theatre is the one licensed room for it; keep it
to three cards, seconds each.

### w9 · sound-of-text — the murmur bed  [cost M]

the piece has no audio. synthesize a murmur **from the text buffer
itself**: each frame's visible letter histogram drives filtered noise
(vowel letters → formant bands, punctuation → ticks); the world
sounds like what it is made of. depth lowers the cutoff (deeper =
duller = underwater); the lamp hums on its own cycle; applause is a
burst of plosives. label: SIMULATED. default quiet, `m` mutes. *root:*
"The moss on the stones to my right whisper of the silent night" —
whispering is already the world's verb; JOUR/A voice memos stay
unused (privacy — his voice does not enter). *risk:* cheap synthesis
cheapens everything — prototype behind a flag; adopt only if it
disappears into the room.

### w10 · provenance — the desk (a secret, not a stage)  [cost M/L]

somewhere off-path (found, never directed-to): the desk where the
dream was written — the 74.5-minute writing session as a strip of
weather you can stand inside, one photographed journal page
(writing_session_001) halftoned into letters on the desk. the dream
contains the room of its own writing. *root:* PROCESS.md timeline;
ANIMA ingest; the DNGs. *risk:* this is the museum move v1 was
convicted of — that's why it must be a secret (rewards the returner,
never interrupts the night). build last, if at all.

**ranking:** w1 · w2 · w3 · w4 · w5 · w6 · w7 · w8 · w9 · w10.
w1+w2 are the spine (choice grammar + chapter structure); w3+w4 are
the frame (who sent it, who's breathing); the rest are texture in
descending necessity.

## 2 · abstractness audit — six stages, cold-read

the pattern behind all six findings: **consequences don't travel.**
each scene's events die inside it (only the pass and the jacket
persist). the fixes below are one object, one being, one absence that
*keep going*. that is what "the story ties together" means
mechanically. second pattern: **nobody moves but you** — autonomy
needs beings with their own errands that cross stage lines.

### gate — fate is an event, not a person

the entry gives her: history ("It's been some time since we last
met"), age ("she's growing old"), a dulled edge ("Her cold glare
doesn't cut me anymore"), a question, a request, a rejection, a
scolding. the scene gives her: a column, a grip, one beat. concrete
fixes, in order of weight:
- **she stoops.** per fold, the column ages — leans, loses a cell of
  height, the glare band dims. "she's growing old" made visible over
  the piece's own lifetime.
- **her pen is missing all night.** she asks "have you got a pen?" —
  the pen-hand writes your card with **no ink** (indented letters,
  dimmer than the world). the absence pays off at the theatre: when
  'Contrarian' lands, the margin already says "if only Fate had
  remembered her pen." a pen drops from the rafters onto the stage,
  too late. one prop, two scenes, cause and effect across the night.
- **the tongue costs something you feel.** after paying, every place
  a voice could go shows your silence — a `…` that condenses and
  scatters unformed wherever speaking would happen (the mage's book,
  the gecko's apology — "I couldn't actually say it without my
  tongue, but my eyes told it anyways"). mute-as-experience, not
  mute-as-rule.

### cloakroom — the coats are wallpaper

storing memories is a flag, not a loss. fix: **one coat is yours.**
on storing, a coat condenses cut from the letters of your own walked
path so far (the memory literally stored, wearing the walk), slightly
too big — "each jacket slightly larger than the next" (17.08.26).
each fold it hangs further down the aisle, bigger. the counter issues
a chit: one small numeral joining the pass. thing + consequence, zero
new prose.

### willow — a rule with no transaction

the pass says "All black cats must be registered with the Mage under
the Willow." there are cats; there is a mage; nothing ever happens
between them. fix: **a black cat follows you** from the cloakroom
(autonomy: her own gait, her own pauses). at the willow she stops at
the mage's book and waits, looking at you. the mage — whose register
is refusal, locked — **refuses the registration.** she is turned away
and walks on with you to the streetlamp: an unregistered black cat,
a rule the night lets stand broken. at the theatre she is in the
audience three rows over and does not look at you (misrecognition).
the middle act gains its throughline being. (the mouse also completes
his errand without you: the old Oak stands past the willow; a tiny
tuxedo arrives late, "so long my friend.")

### streetlamp — company runs one way

the wait works. but you keep him company and he never registers it.
fix: **the glow leans back.** stand with him through a sag and his
next glow shifts a cell toward you, a band warmer. when the ride
takes you, he straightens to watch you go — one posture, no text.
"I could sense him getting tired" — sensing must run both ways.

### theatre — the seat has no texture between beats

true to the entry, the show plays at you and leaving is the only
verb. but the entry leaves a question on the table: "They leave the
pits in the olives here, where should I put it?" fix: **the pit stays
in your hand.** from the sip onward, one `·` rides your row — on the
stairs, in the boat, at the fold. nowhere to put it, never resolved.
a tiny unresolved that travels the rest of the night. (option, for
verdict: at the ledge, the pit is the thing you toss — "I toss a rock
below / There's never a splash." the entry keeps rock and pit
separate; merging them is interpretation. flag, don't assume.)
smaller: the gecko's cigar-smoke should be walked *through* — two
seconds of veil at the row's mouth, a held breath.

### boat — the leap is narrated, not felt

the ledge lines type while you walk; the fall is just a transition.
fix: **the first step off refuses you.** you hang — "I close my eyes
as my feet dangle" — one held breath, then the trumpets and the
stairs float in; the leap only completes from the stairs' end. the
refused fall is the difference between reading about a ledge and
standing on one.

## 3 · submission argument — one page

**claim.** v2's story-mode is not a gamification layer on an artwork;
it is the artwork completing its critical claim. the piece argues
that a self can be *represented without being captured* — v1 built
that argument into materials (verbatim text, honest signals); v2
builds it into **agency**. the faded-hand grammar stages the
interactor's condition inside any modeled system: you may decline
every offered hand, and the night arrives anyway. flavor changes;
fate doesn't. that is the honest shape of life inside predictive
systems — recommendation, profiling, biosignal inference — rendered
as felt form rather than wall text. and it is not a borrowed trope:
the mechanic is transcribed from the author's own journal (17.08.26),
which turns a game pattern into testimony. LO3 (critical issues:
agency, determinism, data-as-destiny) is evidenced *in the mechanics*.

**method continuity.** the constraint system remains the method and
the AI story: agents as medium under authored terms of entry (the
consent-architecture thesis of his network-thinking essay, now
practiced at scale). v2 *widens the corpus, not the rules* — deeper
dialogue and character come from cross-entry mining of his own
journal (the jackets from 17.08, the hands from 17.08, "liminal" from
31.07), every displayed line still his, verbatim, source-tagged in
comments. the parallel-instance process (fable ideation forks, opus
implementation lanes, cross-model consultation if used) is exactly
the iterative documentation the brief rewards — it goes straight into
PROCESS.md as method evidence.

**the EEG's honest role deepens.** progressive liminality couples the
night's stages to the recorded session's own arc — the piece never
renders the subconscious; it lets a recorded night set the weather of
a written one, and says so (`recorded · 06.06.26 · 74 minutes`). the
crossing keeps its discipline (three clocks, none agreeing; no
biosignal claim without its surrogate number). the second dreamer's
breath makes the AI/biosignal link *present* without ever making it a
picture — the anti-anadol stance, upheld under more story, not less.

**lineage to cite (terminal-native, without leaning on likeness):**
the parser tradition (Crowther & Woods' *Adventure*, Infocom) — world
navigated through language; hypertext fiction (Joyce, *afternoon*);
Inkle's *80 Days* (illusion-of-choice craft); *Kentucky Route Zero*
(typographic theatre, walking as reading); *Disco Elysium* (interior
voices as interface — cousin to the margin); the roguelike/ASCII line
(*Rogue* → *Dwarf Fortress*) for character-lattice worlds; plus the
standing canon — Lucier (biosignal as material), Jung's Red Book
(the method's ancestor: one's own night, illuminated by hand),
Herndon (governed instruments), McCarthy (the system performed),
Anadol as counter-example.

**what could weaken it — and the guards:**
1. *gamification cheapens the dream* → no score, no fail state, no
   chrome; every interface element set in the writing's own alphabet;
   choices never branch plot — they tune weather and memory.
2. *content sprawl breaks verbatim-only* → the line holds absolutely:
   if a needed sentence doesn't exist in the corpus, **the mechanic
   changes until it does.** no agent prose, ever; per-line source
   tags; a provenance table lands in PROCESS.md.
3. *the report can't cover a bigger piece in 2000 words* → story-mode
   is the report's gift: describe one walk and the whole argument
   walks with it.
4. *scope* → w1+w2 are submission-strengthening; w7–w10 are not
   submission-critical. if the EC date lands near, the deck freezes
   at the spine.
5. *the repo mandate* — unchanged, still **parked for bobby's word**
   (git init + hosting are his accounts, his call; v2 adds files but
   not cost to that step).
