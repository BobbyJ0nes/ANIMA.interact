## 1. why beings feel one-dimensional (diagnosis)

the streetlamp is the only being that fully works, and the reasons are
structural, not aesthetic:

1. **his life runs without you.** the 26s tired cycle plays whether or not
   anyone watches. every other being is either static (Fate's column) or
   purely reactive to proximity (condense-by-distance).
2. **proximity reveals, behavior engages.** you *see* the lamp by walking
   up; you *engage* him by a behavior — staying, witnessing a full sag.
   Fate today engages on a hitbox (cross the line → pass granted).
3. **he remembers within the mechanic** (sawSag, rideDue). most beings
   remember nothing.

so: one-dimensionality = no idle, hitbox engagement, no memory. the fix is
a grammar, applied per being, not more geometry.

## 2. the autonomy grammar (pattern, not framework)

every being must answer four questions. **recommendation: codify as a
CONTRACT.md checklist + per-scene closures — do NOT build an engine-level
Being class.** the lamp/mouse/ride are already closures over module state;
the pattern generalises by discipline, not machinery (and avoids the
over-architecture failure mode).

- **idle** — what does it do unwatched? must run env-coupled
  (tension/drift/crossing) and indifferent to the camera.
- **notice** — at what distance does its *body* admit you exist
  (turn, brighten, pause), before any words?
- **engage** — its one encounter, gated by a *behavior* of yours
  (stillness, facing, timing, following), never by hitbox alone.
- **remember** — what changes on revisit / per fold? (dream store or
  in-scene state.)

new verbs this unlocks (all input-free — position, gaze, time):
**witness** (stay through a cycle — exists), **decline** (step back /
turn away — the night absorbs it), **follow a gaze** (face what a being
faces), **keep company** (sit near, no goal).
