## what this version is

the 06.06.26 dream-writing as a sequenced night in the termfield renderer:

- **termfield engine** — software typesetting renderer, no three.js. heightfield
  column-march, per-cell char/slot/depth buffers, six registers
  (night/folio/phosphor/rose/page/veil), ten semantic bands, env layer
  (MODEL oscillators / RECORDED eeg session01, honesty-labeled).
- **the night** — director-sequenced dream: gate → cloakroom → willow →
  streetlamp (the wait, the ride) → theatre (the show, the menu, the leap) →
  boat (stillness) → ** curtains close ** → the fold, back to the gate.
  transitions dissolve the world into its own letters (veil/streak/fall).
- **the margin voice** — bobby's writing typed into a cleared band near the
  foot of the frame, ~20 cues at truth-moments. the writing is the tutorial.
- **doctrine** — signs type, beings condense; visitor mute everywhere
  (tongue paid); verbatim prose incl. typos; nothing from nowhere;
  honesty labels LIVE/RECORDED/MODEL/SIMULATED; both crossings shown.
